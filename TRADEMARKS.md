# Project Watt Cubed trademark policy

This policy covers the name “Project Watt Cubed”, official wordmarks and logos,
and other branding that the project designates as identifying an official
Project Watt Cubed release, service, registry, or organisation (collectively,
the “Project Marks”).

The Project Marks identify origin; they do not make the software proprietary.
The software and content licences grant copyright permissions, while this
policy prevents confusion about who produced or endorsed something. Nothing
here limits truthful statements about compatibility or origin.

## Uses that do not require separate permission

You may:

- discuss, review, criticise, teach, or report on Project Watt Cubed;
- say truthfully that software, a mod, a server, or a service is “for”,
  “compatible with”, “based on”, or “forked from” Project Watt Cubed;
- operate a community site, wiki, forum, tutorial, or fan project that is
  clearly labelled unofficial;
- redistribute an unmodified official release under its official name, provided
  you identify the source and do not imply that you are the project or an
  endorsed distributor;
- show the Project Marks as reasonably necessary in screenshots, commentary,
  comparisons, compatibility notices, or attribution; and
- use the name in a repository history or factual credit describing a fork's
  origin.

Commercial activity does not by itself make a truthful use impermissible. For
example, a host may advertise “hosting for Project Watt Cubed” if the wording
and presentation do not imply official status or endorsement.

## Forks and modified distributions

A modified distribution may accurately say that it is based on Project Watt
Cubed, but it must:

- use a clearly distinct primary name and visual identity;
- state prominently that it is unofficial and modified;
- remove or replace official logos and other in-product branding that would
  make users think it is an official build; and
- avoid calling its own service, account system, server list, or mod registry
  the official Project Watt Cubed service.

These requirements do not prevent a fork from preserving copyright notices,
licence notices, historical references, or factual attribution.

## Uses that require permission

Please obtain permission before using a Project Mark:

- as the primary name or logo of a modified product, company, organisation,
  conference, domain, social-media account, merchandise line, or paid service;
- in a way that suggests sponsorship, certification, partnership, or
  endorsement; or
- as a badge claiming “official” status.

## Uses that are not permitted

You may not:

- impersonate the official project, its maintainers, releases, servers, or
  registries;
- present an incompatible or modified build as an unmodified official release;
- use confusingly similar branding to conceal a fork's independent origin; or
- use a Project Mark in a deceptive, fraudulent, or unlawful way.

Fan art and parody are welcome when their context is clearly unofficial and the
work is not used as a misleading source identifier.

## Questions and correction

If a use creates confusion, maintainers may ask for a reasonable correction,
such as adding an “unofficial” label, changing a product name, or removing an
official logo. Permission under this policy ends for a use that remains
materially misleading after a reasonable request to correct it.

Open a repository discussion or contact the maintainers before launch if you
want confirmation about a proposed use. This policy grants no ownership in the
Project Marks and does not claim that every mark is registered.
