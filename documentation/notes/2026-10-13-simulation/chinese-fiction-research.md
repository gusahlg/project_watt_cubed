# China-affiliated research, fictional universes, and two distinct small laws

Date: 2026-10-13. This is research and mathematical criticism, not a new benchmark or a
claim that a final PWC universe has been found. Runtime packages and their actual checks
are recorded separately in the [focused continuation](focused-universes.md). This note writes no runtime
code. It extends [the broad comparison](research.md) and
[the Soviet/Russian investigation](soviet-and-new-models.md).

**Selection:** the strongest additional experiment is bounded, dipole-conserving occupation.
A conditional second experiment is matter whose internal state is an operation, with
noncommuting encounters governed by conjugation. They investigate different questions: **what may move**
and **what an encounter does to identity**. More coefficient variants of waves, spins,
diffusion, or adaptive bonds would offer less new evidence at this stage.

The scientific sources below have an explicitly printed affiliation with an institution in
China. This is an affiliation criterion, not an inference about authors' nationality or
ethnicity. International collaborations qualify where the paper prints such an affiliation.
Several initially promising search results did not qualify: for example, the authors of
*Fracton hydrodynamics without time-reversal symmetry* print Colorado and Stanford
affiliations in that paper, despite search results mentioning a different institutional
association. It is not used as Chinese research evidence here.

**Source result** means the cited authors' evidence or construction. **Derivation** means an
argument presented here. **Proposal** means a PWC law or experiment suggested here. Fiction
is labelled separately and supplies questions and metaphors; it supplies no scientific
validation. No quantum result is silently transferred to a classical integer simulation.

## 1. What the research contributes

### Higher-moment conservation changes mobility, not just transport speed

**Source result.** Begun, Chernodub, Goy, and Molochkov study a compact XY-plaquette model
using Monte Carlo simulation. Their lattice distinguishes two spatial axes and an imaginary
time axis. They find a regime in which vortex trajectories concentrate in spontaneously
located walls, with motion constrained within those walls. Molochkov's printed affiliation
is BIMSA, Tsinghua University, Beijing. The model's polynomial phase-shift symmetry provides
a route to charge and dipole conservation.
[Original paper, PRB 112, 035411, 2025](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/y3yg-zkzv),
[author preprint, 2409.15638](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15638).

Their representative spatial interaction is

```text
S_xy = β_xy ∑ [1 - cos(Δ_x Δ_y φ)],
```

with a separate nearest-neighbor temporal term. This is not a real-time game update rule.
Treating Monte Carlo steps as player time would change the question being simulated.

**Source result.** Wang, Chen, Yan, and Ye's 2026 preprint studies a two-dimensional
dipole-conserving XY model. Wang and Ye print Sun Yat-sen University affiliations; Yan
prints Westlake University and Westlake Institute for Advanced Study. Their analysis and
parallel-tempering simulations identify ordering and vortex unbinding in compact phase
gradients. Ordinary phase correlations and the usual helicity modulus miss the relevant
order. Anisotropic couplings separate transitions in their studied model.
[Original preprint, 2606.25340v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25340).

The spatial law includes terms of the form

```text
-J_x cos(2φ_i - φ_(i-x) - φ_(i+x))
-J_y cos(2φ_i - φ_(i-y) - φ_(i+y))
-2J_xy cos(φ_i + φ_(i+x+y) - φ_(i+x) - φ_(i+y)).
```

**PWC implication.** Second differences offer a small way to impose a genuinely different
constraint. Also, a useful model may require a different measuring instrument: a simple
color or local occupancy view can hide the quantity carrying long-distance order. None of
the quoted phase behavior is established for our bounded-occupation proposal.

**Source result.** Jiali Zhang and Shaoliang Zhang, with Huazhong University of Science and
Technology and Hefei National Laboratory affiliations, study a driven dipole-conserving
Bose-Hubbard chain. Resonance with on-site interactions changes the expansion of dipoles
and fractons through energy absorption from a time-dependent tensor field.
[Original paper, PRB 113, 144305, 2026](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/5vr6-fswh).

**PWC implication.** A conservation label does not settle the complete mobility question.
State space, allowed events, drive, and additional invariants all matter. The drive in this
paper is an explicit external input, not evidence that a closed fictional world gets energy
for free. PWC need not implement tensor electric fields to explore constrained motion.

### Braiding is a productive analogy, but classical labels are not quantum anyons

**Source result.** Xu and collaborators experimentally prepare a Fibonacci string-net
ground state on a superconducting processor and demonstrate creation, braiding, and
fusion operations. The published paper prints Zhejiang, Tsinghua, Nankai, Hefei National
Laboratory, and Shanghai Qi Zhi Institute affiliations. Their realization uses quantum
states and controlled circuits; it is not a spontaneous classical voxel chemistry.
[Original Nature Physics paper, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-024-02529-6),
[author-group account at Tsinghua](https://cqi.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1018/2930.htm).

**PWC implication.** The valuable question is whether encounter order can be a physical
resource. The experiment's universal quantum-computing properties do not establish that
the six-state classical law proposed below computes anything useful, or that it resists
noise. A resemblance between pictures of paths is insufficient.

**Source result.** Li, Wang, and Zhang's 1994 paper constructs matrix solutions of the
Yang-Baxter equation from braid-group representations. The primary publication record
prints Zhejiang University, the Institute of Theoretical Physics of Academia Sinica in
Beijing, and Xinjiang University affiliations. Its abstract and bibliography were checked;
this note does not claim a full-text reconstruction of their matrix construction.
[Original publication record, CTP 21, 167–172](https://ctp.itp.ac.cn/EN/Y1994/V21/I2/167).

**PWC implication.** Algebra can supply consistency conditions for encounters. Our group
conjugation example is derived independently below; it is not their four-by-four matrix
model translated into Rust.

**Source result.** Fu, Zhao, and Wan construct exactly solvable symmetry-enriched string-net
models. Their preprint prints Fudan, Shanghai Research Center for Quantum Sciences, and
Hefei National Laboratory affiliations. Its input includes fusion-category data and
isomorphisms; an explicit example has nonabelian symmetry.
[Original preprint, 2508.08245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08245).

**PWC inference.** This provides a useful warning about mathematical simplicity. A compact
Hamiltonian notation can hide a large amount of algebraic input and quantum-state handling.
Classically storing a string label on every edge does not reproduce a coherent string-net
superposition. The existing parity-loop experiment already investigates the inexpensive
classical constraint; another renamed loop mod would not establish quantum emergence.

### Nonreciprocity produces activity, but its supply must be part of the world

**Source result.** Chen and collaborators use programmable robots and simulations to study
an active mixture: unlike-species alignment and antialignment are nonreciprocal. They find
collective chiral motion and density-dependent behavior. Their paper prints Shanghai Jiao
Tong, Xi'an Jiaotong, Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology, and Northwestern
Polytechnical University affiliations.
[Original PRL 132, 118301, 2024](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.118301).

**Source result.** Zhang and Garcia-Millan study asymmetric pairwise forces and quantify
irreversibility in solvable active-particle cases. Zhang prints a Wenzhou Institute,
University of Chinese Academy of Sciences affiliation. Their activity originates in
microscopic energy injection; in their examples, suitable diffusivities can recover an
equilibrium description despite nonreciprocity.
[Original PR Research 5, L022033, 2023](https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.L022033).

**Source result.** Cheng and Hu, at Beijing Institute of Technology, investigate odd
elasticity realized by piezoelectric material with linear feedback. Their design explicitly
examines external electrical energy exchange.
[Original Science China paper, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11433-021-1756-0).

**Derivation: the hidden pump is easy to expose.** Let a two-component displacement obey
`F = -k u + a J u`, where `J(x,y)=(-y,x)`. The symmetric term integrates to zero around a
closed cycle. The antisymmetric term gives

```text
W = ∮ F·du = a ∮(-y dx + x dy) = 2a × signed enclosed area.
```

This is an attractive microscopic engine law. But if every cycle creates work, an account
of that work belongs in the fundamental rule. A fictional universe can choose a universal
pump; doing so has consequences for scarcity, stability, sleeping cells, and indefinite
activity. Calling the coefficient “material” does not provide a supply mechanism.

**Selection criticism.** A nonreciprocal spin, phase, or elastic-bond experiment could be
worthwhile later. At present it mostly changes a coupling inside existing families. It is
less distinct than moment conservation or operation-valued matter, and may duplicate the
fuel-front/reservoir work once finite driving is accounted for.

### Emergent geometry is not a free algorithm for a voxel engine

**Source result.** Gan, Shu, and Wu, affiliated with Nanchang University, investigate
thermal geometry through a conjectured surface/state correspondence and thermofield
double construction. Their discussion connects geometry with quantum-state structure.
[Original Physics Letters B paper, 2017](https://s3.cern.ch/inspire-prod-files-2/2520d656685d817040f6d3d567d3cae8).

**PWC inference.** This does not establish a sparse, deterministic, classical rule that
builds playable space. Dynamically changing bond strengths within a fixed voxel lattice
is already geometry memory. To claim a distinct “space from events” model, changing
relations must change actual distance, locality, or causal reach. That raises major storage,
collision, meshing, chart, and multiplayer questions. It remains an ambitious future branch,
not a justified second small field experiment in this round.

## 2. Candidate A: matter whose mobility follows from conserved moments

**Proposal.** Add one signed, bounded occupation `q_i ∈ {-1,0,+1}` to each active cell.
Zero is the sleeping background. The law conserves three kinds of quantity in a closed,
Cartesian laboratory:

```text
Q = ∑ q_i                 total signed occupation
P = ∑ r_i q_i             first spatial moment (one component per axis)
E = ∑ q_i²                occupied-site count for this bounded alphabet.
```

`E` is a norm, not a justified physical energy. It is important to state it: some desired
immobility follows from this additional invariant, not from `P` alone. Signs need not mean
electric charge; they mean opposing values of the same fictional quantity.

### One event follows from linear algebra

For three collinear cells at `r`, `r+d`, `r+2d`, set

```text
v = (1,-2,1)
L = q_0 - 2q_1 + q_2
a = -L/3
q' = q + a v.
```

Apply the event only if `L` is divisible by three and the result remains in the occupation
alphabet; otherwise leave the cells unchanged. This is the integer restriction of an
orthogonal reflection, `q' = q - 2(v·q)/(v·v) v`.

**Derivation.** `∑v=0`, so `Q` is unchanged. Also,
`r - 2(r+d) + (r+2d)=0`, so every component of `P` is unchanged. The reflection preserves
`q·q`, so `E` is unchanged. Applying it twice gives the original vector. Integrality and
alphabet refusal preserve this involution: a valid image maps back; an invalid move is an
identity operation. Refusing an event is not clipping its result.

The rule derives a mobile pattern without naming it:

```text
(+1,-1, 0) ↔ (0,+1,-1).
```

A neutral adjacent pair can translate along its separation axis. An isolated unit cannot
move or split under this closed law: `E=1` allows only one occupied site, while `P` fixes its
position. There is no particle registry containing “immobile charge” and “mobile molecule.”

### Two necessary counterexamples

**Counterexample 1: moments alone do not imply permanent isolation.** With a larger integer
alphabet, the same reflection admits

```text
(3,0,0) → (2,2,-1),
```

which preserves `Q`, `P`, and the quadratic norm. Even charge-plus-dipole conservation
without the norm can permit compensating pair creation. The correct general statement is
that translating a charged object while leaving everything else unchanged changes `P`.
An environment may compensate. “Every isolated charge is eternally frozen” is too broad.

**Counterexample 2: neutral translation being permitted by invariants does not mean our
events implement it.** Straight three-cell events give longitudinal mobility. Translating
an isolated neutral pair transversely also preserves `Q`, `P`, and `E`, but requires a
different neighborhood. For square corners `A,B,C,D` at `(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)`, use

```text
v = (1,-1,-1,1),  a = -(v·q)/2,  q' = q + a v.
(+1,-1,0,0) ↔ (0,0,+1,-1).
```

The same reflection formula proves the invariants. The staged dipole prototype includes
both stencils; its actual check results belong in the separate verification record.
Even with these events, the moment of an isolated neutral pair fixes its separation vector;
neutrality does not grant arbitrary rotation. Additional objects can exchange moment.

### What might become gameplay

**Proposal, unvalidated.** Players could discover that some deposits store stationary
signed occupation while matched pairs carry it. Combining excitations might permit transport,
unlock a jam, or move an otherwise trapped pattern. An instrument measuring `Q`, `P`, and
occupied count would reveal why two visually similar preparations behave differently.

This would be useful if a player can prepare a repeatable experiment, build a path that
routes a mobile pattern, interact with a stored pattern, and leave a persistent readable
result. Conservation by itself promises none of these. Severe fragmentation can produce
excellent memory and terrible machinery: a world that never changes is cheap but not rich.

The existing thirteen experiments include conserved packets, reversible maps, spins, loops,
and block permutations. This law's distinction is an additional spatial invariant that
changes allowed motion according to collective state. It does not merely change propagation
speed or add a new color to the packet alphabet.

### Cost and architecture

One cell's mathematical state needs two bits, although coordinates and sparse-map overhead
will dominate the prototype. Events use a constant-size neighborhood and exact small integer
arithmetic. Empty triples are exact fixed points. Frozen isolated sites can sleep until a
neighbor changes, but the scheduler must retain enough spatial phase to awaken them correctly.
Mobile neutral activity does not settle automatically and continues to cost work.

Use disjoint event partitions with a saved phase, or explicitly define an ordered event
law. Budgeting part of a neighborhood and committing partial updates breaks the reflection.
Budgeting part of a full partition changes the universe unless that event order is itself the
law and its cursor is saved. A complete partition need not require scanning the entire world:
active neighborhoods can be indexed, with wakeup and phase semantics proved separately.

`P` depends on a physical position chart, not PWC's four-dimensional resource coordinates.
Local affine Cartesian events preserve it algebraically. Curved atlas seams, rotations,
periodic wraps, and changing reference frames need an explicit definition. For a translation
of origin, `P` changes by a constant multiple of `Q`; conserved differences remain coherent.
On a periodic laboratory, ordinary coordinate moments are not globally defined; modulo
moments give weaker constraints. Limit the first comparison to a chart interior.

Editing a block must not silently teleport its occupation. Choosing whether a placed block
carries, displaces, or leaves behind `q` is part of the law. Injection explicitly changes
the relevant invariants; a moment-neutral dipole source still adds occupied count. A
“closed” experiment must account for these tool actions and boundary fluxes.

No per-cell field replication is currently supplied by the shared host. Exact arithmetic
helps deterministic replay but does not replicate state, scheduler phase, injections, or
chunk activation. Keep the new experiment singleplayer until a dedicated contract exists.

## 3. Candidate B: relational frames, with activity from noncommutation

**Conditional proposal.** Let each cell hold an element of `S3`, the six permutations of
three abstract marks. Identity `e` is background. A permutation is stored and composed as
a tiny operation, not assigned a named material behavior. For an ordered adjacent pair,
consider the primitive

```text
R(a,b) = (b, b⁻¹ a b).
```

Composition convention must be fixed in code and documentation. The interaction uses one
formula for all six elements and their combinations. The selected experiment adds a
universal eligibility condition:

```text
T(a,b) = R(a,b) if ab ≠ ba; otherwise T(a,b) = (a,b).
```

The package is called `physics-frames`, not a braid or anyon model. The condition supplies
quiescent matter that responds to disagreement between relational frames. There is no list
of authored collision outcomes or named carrier materials. This predicate is our design
choice, not a result of the cited Chinese quantum research.

### Exact claims, proved independently of the quantum papers

**Derivation: inverse.** For output `(x,y)`, the previous state is

```text
R⁻¹(x,y) = (x y x⁻¹, x).
```

For `T`, apply this inverse only to noncommuting outputs; commuting outputs are unchanged.
Eligibility is invariant under the primitive: `a` commutes with `b` exactly when `b`
commutes with `b⁻¹ab`. The inactive sector therefore cannot receive an active image. This
proves the piecewise law is bijective, rather than merely assuming a reversible primitive
remains reversible after arbitrary gating.

**Derivation: local product.** The new ordered product is
`b(b⁻¹ab)=ab`. This gives a chain invariant when encounters act on adjacent entries of a
fixed ordered chain. It does not define a conserved product over arbitrary three-dimensional
face matchings: noncommutative multiplication requires an order.

**Derivation: classes.** Conjugation preserves conjugacy class. The pair's class multiset
therefore remains unchanged. In `S3`, these classes are identity, the three transpositions,
and the two three-cycles. Different transpositions can rewrite one another's labels while
preserving their class. Any emergent “species” would follow from algebraic equivalence,
not a hardcoded list of wire, gate, catalyst, and inert block types.

**Derivation: braid consistency of the rejected unrestricted precursor.** Apply `R` chronologically to an
ordered triple. Both sequences `R12,R23,R12` and `R23,R12,R23` produce

```text
(c, c⁻¹ b c, c⁻¹ b⁻¹ a b c).
```

This is the set-theoretic braid/Yang-Baxter identity for `R`. It equates those particular
sequences; it does not make every encounter order interchangeable. The commutation-gated
law `T` does **not** satisfy the identity in general: the parent investigation found 48
failing triples among the 216 `S3` triples. This exact count is an algebra check reported by
the implementation investigation, not a performance or gameplay measurement. Consequently
the new package must not be advertised as preserving Yang-Baxter consistency or quantum
braiding. Giving up that identity buys stationary carrier arrangements.

An explicit counterexample makes the tradeoff visible. Let `s=(12)`, `t=(23)`, `u=(13)`.
Starting from `(e,s,t)`, chronological `T12,T23,T12` gives `(e,t,u)`, whereas
`T23,T12,T23` gives `(e,u,s)`. Refusal to move through identity means the first sequence
applies the active encounter once and the second applies it twice.

**Derivation: a strong finite-size limitation.** Write `g=ab`. Two encounters simultaneously
conjugate both entries by `g`:

```text
R²(a,b) = (g⁻¹ a g, g⁻¹ b g).
```

Consequently `R^(2m)` is identity when `g` has order `m`. In `S3`, active pair trajectories
have small periods bounded by two, four, or six depending on `g`; some periods are shorter.
Commuting pairs under `T` are fixed points.
A repeated two-cell state is therefore weak evidence for usable oscillators. Richness must
come from larger arrangements and encounters, and has not been demonstrated by this proof.

### What is distinct, and what is still prescribed

The new idea is that encounters rewrite internal operations in a coherent, order-dependent
way. This differs from merely swapping fixed labels between cells. However, the whole
finite reversible update is mathematically a permutation of its global state space, just
as every finite reversible model is. It should not be advertised as escaping that fact.

The unrestricted precursor had `R(a,e)=(e,a)` and `R(e,a)=(a,e)`: a lone operation followed
scheduler-defined paths through identity. This failed to provide stationary matter. In
`T`, every operation commutes with identity, so identity surroundings remain inert. A
region of mutually commuting frames is likewise stationary. A noncommuting preparation
can change adjacent labels in that stationary carrier; it must demonstrate signal motion
through those changes rather than transport through empty space.

The first staged fixture prepares a homogeneous frame, then conjugates one cell by another
frame to introduce a disagreement. Preparation and conjugation are explicit external input.
No claim is made that this carrier is assembled spontaneously from initially identical
vacuum. Regions containing mutually commuting subsets can supply different stationary
backgrounds without a named-material exception, but useful routing and confinement remain
to be discovered. Fixed matchings still influence encounter timing and anisotropy.

Simultaneously conjugating every label by the same `h` is a covariance of this law.
Independent local frame changes require edge transport data to compare neighboring frames.
The six-state cell toy alone is not a complete gauge theory. It has no quantum superposition,
no Fibonacci fusion space, no proven topological error protection, no physical energy or
momentum account, and no demonstrated universal computation.

### Cost, discoverability, and fair criticism

State needs three bits in principle. Permutation composition and inversion are bounded,
small operations; commuting regions are exact fixed points. Sparse event wakeup and
saved matching phase matter as much as in Candidate A. The single event is local and
reversible, but scheduling, state-dependent eligibility, clipping, and world edits can
destroy reversibility even when the algebraic primitive is correct.

**Proposal, unvalidated.** A player could send one operation through a structure, send
another in a different order, and compare an observable result. This offers memory from
encounter history. A successful demonstrator should preserve a readable result and repeat
after reset without relying on an authored “detector block.” Merely showing six colored
labels exchanging places is insufficient.

Composition-driven rates or orientation changes are possible later, but must be checked for
reversible admission and resource-space seam dependence. Keeping the first primitive simple
provides a clear baseline against which to criticize such additions.

## 5. Original directions to explore inside these laws

These are independent proposals, not sourced results or extra recommended mod variants.

**Moment-bearing machines.** A tool could manipulate a trapped occupation by exchanging
moment with another part of the same structure. The machine's recoil would be in its
occupation arrangement rather than necessarily its voxel position. This might make a
“battery” a spatial preparation rather than a scalar counter. Test whether usable capacity
is finite and whether it can be recharged through the same law. Do not exempt a tool from
the moment ledger merely to obtain the desired outcome.

**A material's transportability could be its collective identity.** Instead of declaring a
block solid or conductive, classify localized preparations by the events they admit under
`Q,P,E`. A deposit with the same total amount could be locked, mobile, or able to trade
moment with neighbors depending on arrangement. The first prototype may be too frozen;
that would be a result against it, not a reason to add named exceptions.

**History as chemistry.** In operation-valued matter, assembling the same participants in
a different order can produce different labels. Players might engineer an encounter path
that writes information into a reusable arrangement. The challenge is finding an output
that survives continued evolution and is readable through a generic interaction. A HUD
display is an investigation instrument, not evidence that an in-universe sensor exists.

**Geometry as a later branch, not another label.** If encounters eventually change which
sites are genuinely adjacent, an operation could rewrite its own transmission geometry.
Then distance would be the number of available interactions along a path. This would be
distinct from a spring coefficient only if changed adjacency alters actual causal reach.
Unbounded new edges destroy sparse locality; degree bounds, communication latency, and
safe embedding would be fundamental commitments. Do not hide those commitments in core.

## 6. The smallest useful evidence to request later

The user asked for little testing now. The following is a validation ladder, not a claim
that all of it has been performed, and not a reason to postpone ideas until exhaustive tests.

| Stage | Candidate A | Candidate B |
|---|---|---|
| Algebra | Exact `Q,P,E`; admitted reflection is its inverse | Exact inverse, eligibility invariance, class counts, local ordered product; explicitly reject braid identity for the gated law |
| Small closed run | Isolated unit stationary, a neutral pair genuinely translating under the chosen schedule, honest movement direction | A conflict changes an internal label and reaches beyond its injected site through a stationary commuting carrier |
| Architectural inspection | Whole events, persisted phase, bounded input, chart-safe neighborhoods | Disjoint ordered encounters, persisted phase, no silent clamp or inconsistent composition convention |
| First gameplay probe | Prepare, transport, interact, retain, measure, reset | Route, write a durable result, read through generic interaction, reset |
| Performance comparison | Work per active neighborhood and frozen world memory | Work per active encounter and continuing background traffic |
| Multiplayer work, later | Replicate/replay occupation, moment-accounted actions, and scheduling | Replicate/replay labels, ordered encounters, actions, and scheduling |

Neither the mobility proof nor the conjugation algebra establishes a productive technology tree.
They justify two sharply scoped experiments. Which law deserves a larger world should be
decided by constructions players can discover, the cost of keeping them running, and how
many exceptions are required when the first construction fails.
