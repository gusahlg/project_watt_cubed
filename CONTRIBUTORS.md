to Claude Fable 5 and Claude Opus 4.8, and subsequent development has used
OpenAI Codex. These systems are recorded as tools, not as copyright holders or
legal contributors.

The human who submits an AI-assisted change is responsible for reviewing it,
confirming the right to submit it, disclosing material assistance, and signing
off the commit under the DCO.
