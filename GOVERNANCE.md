# Project Watt Cubed Governance

## Purpose

Project Watt Cubed exists to produce a completely free-software voxel game
and ecosystem. People and companies may use it commercially, but official
development must preserve every user's freedom to study, modify, redistribute,
sell, and host the software and content.

## Permanent principles

1. Project-owned software remains under a strong copyleft free-software
   licence, currently `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.
2. Project-owned artistic and written material remains free culture, currently
   `CC-BY-SA-4.0`.
3. The project issues no proprietary licensing exceptions and does not
   dual-license project-owned components for proprietary distribution.
4. Contributors retain their copyright. The project requires DCO sign-offs,
   not copyright assignment or a proprietary-relicensing CLA.
5. Proprietary dependencies, accounts, SDKs, or services may not become
   mandatory for building, modifying, hosting, or meaningfully extending the
   official experience.
6. Official mods and network services must provide corresponding source under
   the applicable policy and licence.
7. Weakening copyleft requires permission from every affected copyright
   holder; a maintainer or community vote cannot replace that permission.
8. Trademark control may prevent confusion, impersonation, and false claims
   of endorsement, but may not prevent truthful descriptions of forks or
   their origin.
9. Security information may be held under a time-limited, need-to-know
   embargo. Fixes and advisories are published under the normal project
   licences once coordinated disclosure is safe.
10. Maintainers are accountable to contributors and may be removed through
    the process below.

## Roles

Contributors submit code, content, review, testing, design, or community work
under the project policies.

Maintainers review and merge contributions, make releases, administer project
infrastructure, and enforce published policies. These powers do not convey
ownership of contributor copyrights and cannot authorise proprietary
relicensing.

Infrastructure and trademark stewards administer credentials and official
identity. They must follow this governance document and the separate trademark
policy.

## Decisions

Technical decisions should be made transparently and by rough consensus,
supported by tests, measurements, compatibility requirements, and the
project's purpose. When consensus is not possible, non-conflicted maintainers
may decide by a simple majority and must record the decision and material
objections.

No vote may override a copyright holder's rights, the terms of an existing
licence, or the permanent principles above.

## Maintainer appointment and removal

A contributor may nominate a maintainer based on sustained, constructive
project work. Existing non-conflicted maintainers appoint the nominee by
consensus or, failing that, a two-thirds majority after a public comment
period.

Any contributor may open a documented removal proposal for persistent policy
violations, abuse of access, prolonged abandonment, or conduct that materially
harms the project. The subject must receive:

- the reasons and supporting evidence;
- a reasonable opportunity to respond;
- at least fourteen days of public community comment, unless an immediate
  credential suspension is necessary for security; and
- a decision by non-conflicted maintainers after considering contributor
  feedback.

Removal requires consensus or a two-thirds majority of non-conflicted
maintainers. If no non-conflicted maintainer can decide, contributors with
accepted project contributions select three temporary, non-conflicted
reviewers by public nomination; two of those three must agree. Credential
suspension may precede the final decision when necessary to protect users or
infrastructure, but does not predetermine removal.

## Amendments

Governance amendments require public notice, at least fourteen days for
comment, and a two-thirds maintainer majority. Amendments cannot retroactively
change existing licence grants or bypass the unanimous copyright-holder
permission required to weaken copyleft.
